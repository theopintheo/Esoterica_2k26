ESOTERICA 2K26 is designed to foster technical innovation and offer a high-octane 8-hour sprint where passionate student developers transform impactful ideas into real-world software solutions.

Problem Statements

[ TRACK 01 ]
restaurant

> CANTEEN ORDER SYSTEM

Build a mobile app where students can browse menus, pre-order meals, and select pickup time slots. The canteen receives orders in advance, prepares food on time, and notifies students when ready.

[ TRACK 02 ]
search

> FIND MISSING ITEMS

Create a mobile app where users can upload photos of found items. Use image recognition to match lost item descriptions with found items. Include geolocation, notifications, and admin verification.

[ TRACK 03 ]
event

> District-Wide College Event Platform

A centralized digital ecosystem where institutions easily publish events and students can universally discover and register for cross-campus opportunities in one place.

[ TRACK 04 ]
work

> Skill-Based Freelance Marketplace

A dedicated student marketplace that connects emerging talent with real projects, enabling them to showcase portfolios, offer services, and establish professional credibility.

[ TRACK 05 ]
bloodtype

> Smart Blood Donor Network

To create a seamless, real-time platform that instantly connects hospitals and patients with nearby, available blood donors to save lives without delay.

[ TRACK 06 ]
volunteer_activism

> Food Donation Platform

To create a simple web platform that reduces food waste and fights hunger by instantly connecting local restaurants with nearby orphanages, making food donation fast and effortless.

> Eligibility & Team Formation

  • [✓] Only Undergraduate (UG) students are eligible to participate. All departments are included.
  • [✓] Each team must consist of 2–3 members.
  • [✓] Cross-college teams are allowed.
  • [✓] Every participant must carry a valid College ID Card or Bonafide Certificate.
  • [✓] All team members are collectively responsible for complying with the hackathon rules.

> Registration

  • [✓] Registration fee is ₹150 per participant.
  • [✓] Registration details provided by participants must be accurate and complete.

> Domains & Track Selection

  • [✓] The hackathon will provide 6 domains.
  • [✓] Each domain will contain 3 tracks.
  • [✓] Each team must select one domain.
  • [✓] From the selected domain, the team must choose one challenge.
  • [✓] The project must remain relevant to the selected track/problem statement.

> Development Requirements

Before the Competition

  • [✓] Teams are expected to complete approximately 50% of their project before the hackathon.
  • [✓] Teams may prepare the initial codebase, project structure, basic architecture, and foundation beforehand.
  • [✓] Pre-event work must not constitute a fully completed project.

During the Competition

  • [✓] The remaining 50% of the project must be developed during the hackathon.
  • [✓] Teams must demonstrate genuine development and implementation during the event.
  • [✓] Pre-built or previously completed projects presented as hackathon work will not be accepted.
  • [✓] An Online Round will take place before the competition to filter the work completed.

> AI Usage Policy

  • [✓] AI tools are allowed during the hackathon.
  • [✓] Teams may use AI tools for coding assistance, debugging, research, brainstorming, documentation, design, and other development-related tasks.
  • [✓] Participants are responsible for understanding and being able to explain all AI-assisted code and implementations used in their project.
  • [✓] Using AI does not exempt participants from demonstrating their own technical knowledge and contribution.
  • [✓] AI-generated content must not involve plagiarism, copyright infringement, malicious code, or unethical practices.
  • [✓] Teams must be able to explain their project's architecture, functionality, technologies, and implementation during the judging and Q&A.

> Project Submission Requirements

  • [✓] Every team must have a working project/prototype.
  • [✓] Proper project documentation.
  • [✓] A presentation ready for evaluation.
  • [✓] Complete project code uploaded to GitHub.
  • [✓] A live demonstration of the working project.
  • [✓] A backup screen recording demonstrating the working project in case of technical issues.

> Presentation & Demonstration

  • [✓] Each team will receive a total of 8 minutes.
  • [✓] 5 minutes will be allocated for the presentation.
  • [✓] 3 minutes will be allocated for Q&A.
  • [✓] Every team member must participate in the presentation.
  • [✓] No extra time will be given to any team.
  • [✓] Teams must be prepared to demonstrate their project within the allotted time.

Presentation should cover: Introduction, Problem Statement, Proposed Solution, Justify Your Challenge, Architecture/Workflow, Technologies Used, Development Process, Working Prototype Demonstration, What Makes the Solution Different, Benefits & Target Users, Scalability, Future Scope.

> Judging Criteria

  • 1. Innovation – Originality and creativity of the solution.
  • 2. Problem Solving – Effectiveness and practicality of the proposed solution.
  • 3. Technical Implementation – Quality, functionality, and effectiveness of the implementation.
  • 4. Project Knowledge – Understanding of the architecture, code, technologies, and implementation decisions; ability to explain AI-assisted components.
  • 5. Demonstration & Presentation – Clarity of presentation, quality of live demonstration, and ability to communicate the project's value.

> Code of Conduct

  • [✓] Participants must maintain respectful and professional behaviour throughout the event.
  • [✓] Cheating and plagiarism are strictly prohibited.
  • [✓] Downloaded, copied, or previously completed repositories/projects will not be accepted.
  • [✓] Participants must not engage in illegal, harmful, malicious, or unethical activities.
  • [✓] Any form of misconduct may result in disqualification.
  • [✓] If any member violates the rules, the entire team may be disqualified.
  • [✓] Teams must respect organizers, judges, volunteers, and fellow participants.

> Judging & Disqualification

  • [✓] Judging will be conducted fairly and without bias.
  • [✓] The judges' decision will be final and binding.
  • [✓] Organizers reserve the right to disqualify teams that violate the rules.
  • [✓] Teams must be able to demonstrate genuine knowledge of their project, regardless of whether AI tools were used.

> Food & Certificates

  • [✓] Lunch and snacks will be provided.
  • [✓] Participant certificates will be provided.

> Important Reminders

  • [✓] Bring your College ID Card or Bonafide Certificate.
  • [✓] Keep your project functional and ready for demonstration.
  • [✓] Keep your GitHub repository updated.
  • [✓] Keep the presentation within the 5-minute limit.
  • [✓] Ensure every team member participates.
  • [✓] Keep a backup screen recording of the working project.
  • [✓] Be prepared for the 3-minute Q&A session.
  • [✓] AI tools may be used, but every team member must understand their project and be able to explain its implementation.
  • [✓] No extra time will be provided.

Final Note: By participating in the hackathon, all teams agree to follow these rules and regulations. Any violation may result in team-wide disqualification. The organizers and judges reserve the right to make the final decision regarding rule violations, project eligibility, and judging.
